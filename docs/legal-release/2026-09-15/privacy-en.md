# Privacy and Data Collection Notice

2026-09-15.1

## Operator and contact

Operator: YUL HEO · officialscoor@gmail.com. This notice describes processing for accounts, entries and public participation in Scoor.

## Essential account data

Account IDs, email, authentication data, nickname, name/profile information supplied by login providers, and consent versions, choices and timestamps are used for login, account services and consent records. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

## Information you choose to provide

Scores and dates, optional notes, bio/avatar, manually chosen country, public posts, comments, World participation, likes, proposals, reports, blocks and support messages are processed for those features. Optional notes, bios and photos are not required for a basic account.

## Device, server and public visibility

Entries and settings are stored on the device; authentication tokens use Keychain. Signed-in records synchronize with Supabase. Existing device records are not migrated and sent to an account before required consent. Others can see content you choose to publish; private journaling is distinct from Home sharing and public submissions.

## Providers and locations

Authentication, databases and APIs use Supabase Pte. Ltd. (privacy contact: privacy@supabase.io). The primary database is in Seoul, South Korea. Support, security and subprocessors are not necessarily limited to Korea. Inquiry email addresses and messages are received and answered using Google Gmail. Apple/Google sign-in is also subject to their policies. We process data needed for account services and public features you choose; user entries are not provided for advertising sales or external AI training.

## Retention and deletion

Accounts and content are retained while their features are provided. Deleting an entry also unshares its linked Home post. Once a deletion reaches the server, deleted text, scores and mood content are cleared. Minimal synchronization markers (account ID, entry date and deletion timestamp) remain while the account exists to prevent old devices from restoring deleted entries. Deleted post/comment relationship markers and withdrawn or rejected proposals are cleared within 30 days. Account deletion removes associated entries, posts and approved topics. Failed Apple token revocations are retried through a restricted outbox. Copies saved by others or device backups may not disappear at the same time.

## Sensitive information is separate

An everyday score is not a medical diagnosis or automatically health information. Designated topics revealing sensitive information, such as political opinions, require separate optional consent. Scores, comments and account IDs are used to publish opinions and aggregate results until you delete the participation, withdraw consent or close your account. Anonymous display remains linked to your account on the server. Withdrawal in Settings also deletes participation in those topics. Journaling and ordinary topics remain available without this consent. Do not enter sensitive information or other people’s personal data in other fields.

## Refusal, withdrawal and rights

Declining essential account consent prevents registration, synchronization and publishing. You may omit optional information. Settings lets you withdraw required consent, sign out or request account deletion. Contact the operator for access, correction, erasure or restriction. Withdrawing consent alone does not delete previously published content.

## Tracking, notifications and changes

The current app does not bundle advertising SDK or cross-app tracking consent into this flow. Notifications use separate iOS permissions and settings. Documents are versioned; material changes request renewed agreement. Contact officialscoor@gmail.com with privacy questions.

## Access and diagnostic logs

Supabase retains access and authentication logs for security and troubleshooting. These may include timestamps, request paths and response status, app/OS information, IP addresses and account identifiers. We do not use them for advertising tracking. Retention depends on the operational service settings and security needs.
